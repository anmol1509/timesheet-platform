The challenge

Generic HR software was never built for manpower supply.

Supplying hundreds of workers to client sites is a different business from employing them in one office. Most HRMS and ERP tools stop at employee records and payroll, so everything else ends up in spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and inboxes.

Timesheets in every format

Hours arrive from each site on paper, in Excel and as phone photos. Someone retypes them for payroll, then again for billing.

Documents that expire quietly

Visas, Emirates IDs, passports and labour cards lapse between renewals. By the time anyone notices, the fine has arrived.

Payroll built by hand

Overtime, deductions, loans and the WPS file are worked out in spreadsheets every month, for hundreds of workers at once.

Hours worked, never billed

When timesheets and invoices live in separate files, approved hours slip through and revenue quietly leaks away.

A workforce spread across camps and sites

Beds, buses and site rotations are coordinated by phone, with no single view of who is where today.

Subcontractors on email

Supplier crews, their timesheets and their payments are tracked through forwarded spreadsheets and follow-up calls.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from a general HRMS or ERP?

General systems stop at employee records and payroll. Manpower suppliers also need client timesheets, billing by the hour, visa and ID tracking, camps, transport, mobilisation and subcontractor crews. Those are built in here rather than bolted on.

What does getting started involve?

Employees and sites are imported from your existing sheets, and your current timesheet workbook can be uploaded as-is. There is nothing to re-key before your first month.

Can we bring our existing Excel timesheets?

Yes. Upload the consolidated workbook and each month tab is detected automatically. Re-uploading a corrected file updates that month instead of creating duplicates.

Does payroll produce a WPS file our bank accepts?

Payroll runs export a WPS salary information file using each employee's bank and routing details, ready to upload to your bank or exchange house.

Do workers need to install an app?

No. The employee portal runs in any phone browser, so workers can check payslips, attendance and documents without downloading anything.

Who can see salary and client data?

Access is controlled by role and branch, so each person only sees the modules they need. Every create, edit and delete is recorded in the audit log.

How do subcontractors fit in?

Your suppliers get their own portal to receive demands, submit workers and timesheets, and follow their payments, all without access to your internal data.
