# Demo Script (read aloud)

Lines in plain text are spoken. **[Click]** lines are actions. Pause where you see *(pause)*. Replace **[Name]** and **[Company]**. Target: 30 minutes.

---

## Opening (2 min)

Thank you for your time, **[Name]**. Before I show anything, let me say what I heard from you.

You supply workers to contractors across the UAE. Your margin is made or lost between four moments: a client asks for people, you find and deploy them, you record the hours, and you bill and pay. Today those moments live in Excel, WhatsApp and phone calls.

I will follow one worker's journey through all four, in about thirty minutes. Please stop me any time you see something that matches a headache you have.

---

## 1. Dashboard (2 min)

**[Click: Dashboards]**

This is what the owner sees first thing in the morning. Workforce, billing, timesheets and demand, each on its own tab.

**[Click: Workforce, then the compliance runway]**

This strip shows visas, Emirates IDs and labour cards expiring in the next 30, 60 and 90 days. Fines for a missed renewal are avoidable, and this makes them visible *before* the date.

*(pause)* How do you track expiries today?

## 2. Profile (1 min)

**[Click: Profile]**

Every user has their own profile and can choose which dashboard they land on. The finance person can open on billing, operations on workforce.

## 3. Employee (4 min)

**[Click: Employees, then New]**

Let me register a worker. Notice the Emirates ID field formats itself, and the phone, email and IBAN are checked as I type, so bad data does not get in.

**[Click: Save as draft]**

If I get called away, I save as a draft and finish later from the Drafts page.

**[Click: open an existing worker]**

Here is a worker's full file: visa and labour card history, where he has been deployed, documents, notes, and even the PPE and uniform issued to him.

**[Click: Renewals]**

And this is your PRO's daily list. Everything expiring, for whom, in one screen.

## 4. Letter (1.5 min)

**[Click: Letters, generate an NOC]**

Need a NOC or an experience letter? Pick the template, pick the worker, and it prints on your letterhead with your stamp. Ten seconds, and every letter is recorded.

## 5. Supplier (2 min)

**[Click: Suppliers]**

You also use sub-suppliers when you are short. They live here with their contacts, bills and workers.

**[Click: switch to the supplier portal tab]**

This is what *they* see. They log in, submit their available workers, send offers and invoices, and message you. No more chasing by phone.

## 6. Client (1.5 min)

**[Click: Clients]**

Your clients, their contacts, TRN and agreed rates per trade. Every invoice later reads from here, so rates are never retyped.

## 7. Project (1.5 min)

**[Click: Projects, open one]**

Each project ties together the client, the people assigned, the contacts, the holidays and the LPO. Every hour and every dirham from here on is attached to a project.

## 8. Site (1 min)

**[Click: Sites]**

Projects can have several sites. You always know which worker is where.

## 9. Demand (4 min)

**[Click: Demand, open a request]**

A client asks for twenty masons and ten helpers. It lands here as a demand request.

**[Click: Approve partially]**

You approve what you can supply today. For the rest, you request offers from suppliers.

**[Click: Offers, then Allocation]**

Offers come in, you allocate workers, and the request moves through mobilisation, site arrival and, at the end, demobilisation. At any point you can answer "where is this order?" in one click.

**[Click: demand pack PDF]**

And the pack for the client is one button.

## 10. Facilities (3 min)

**[Click: Accommodation, Camps]**

Your camps, rooms and even bunk beds with upper and lower berths. Occupancy at a glance.

**[Click: Check in]**

Check a worker in to your own camp, a supplier's, or a client's. If he moves rooms, I switch him and the history is kept.

**[Click: Transport, Inventory]**

Routes and vehicles by project, and PPE and uniform stock. Empty beds and empty seats are money you are paying for.

## 11. Timesheet (3 min)

**[Click: Attendance]**

Hours are recorded daily. If a supervisor made a mistake, the correction goes to an approver, not into a chat.

**[Click: Timesheets, then Client timesheet]**

At month end, approved attendance rolls into the timesheet your client signs. Then:

**[Click: Invoices, open one]**

The tax invoice, with your TRN and VAT, generated from the same hours. The key point: *one source of hours* feeds the invoice and the payroll, so they always agree.

## 12. Upload data (2 min)

**[Click: Upload]**

You may be thinking, "we have years of data in Excel." Good news: you do not throw it away.

**[Drag in the consolidated timesheet workbook]**

Each month tab is detected automatically. It creates the suppliers and clients it finds and loads every worker's hours.

**[Re-upload the same file]**

If I upload it again, it refreshes. No duplicates.

**[Click: Documents, drop in the specimen passport]**

Passports and IDs can be scanned: name, number, date of birth and expiry are read for you, and you confirm. And clients and suppliers can be imported by CSV.

## 13. Finance (3 min)

**[Click: Payroll, open a draft run]**

Payroll runs by company, with loans, repayments and adjustments. Before it is approved, the system warns you about people with no hours or hours that are not yet approved.

**[Click: export SIF]**

Then the WPS file for the bank. We will confirm your bank's exact format together in the pilot.

**[Click: Finance, Expenses, Bills]**

Expenses, supplier bills with part payments, petty cash and budgets, with approvals before money moves.

## 14. Permission (1.5 min)

**[Click: Settings, Roles]**

You decide who sees what. Create a role, tick the modules.

**[Click: Audit log]**

And every change is logged: who changed this rate, and when. That ends the "who did that" arguments.

## 15. Search (30 sec)

**[Press Cmd/Ctrl+K]**

From anywhere, one shortcut. Type a worker's name or ID, or "raise demand", and jump straight there.

## 16. AI assistant (1.5 min)

**[Open the assistant, type: "Which workers' visas expire this month?"]**

You can also just ask in plain English. It reads your live data and gives you the answer with links. For your new staff, this replaces a week of training.

---

## Close (3 min)

Let me play back what we saw: a client request becomes a deployed worker, becomes recorded hours, becomes an invoice and a payroll, with compliance watched the whole way.

What I would propose is a short pilot, two to four weeks, on one branch or one client contract. We load your real workers, clients and rates, and run one cycle alongside what you do today.

To start, I need two people from your side: someone who owns your worker and client data, and someone from finance to check the invoice and the WPS file.

*(pause)* Is there anything you have seen today that would stop you from moving forward? *(listen)*

Then let us book the follow-up. I will send a summary and a pilot plan within three working days.
